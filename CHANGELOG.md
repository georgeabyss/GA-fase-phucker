# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (beta)

First release. macOS (VST3, Audio Unit) and Windows (VST3).

- **One, two or three bands**, each in L / R or M / S, with on / off and solo; the splits keep
  1.5 times apart. Every band has all four sections.
- **Disperse:** up to 1000 all-pass stages, Behaved (smooth) or Unhinged (glitchy when moved), with
  Jitter, Freq, Pinch, Spread and Mix.
- **Distortion:** Noise, Saturate, Clip, Fold, Rectify, Crush, before or after Disperse, with Bias,
  Phat (the low mids and mids), Tone and Mix; the only part that is oversampled.
- **Feedback** around the all-pass chain, with Time, Tone, Invert and Mix; self-oscillates past
  about 80 %.
- **Env Follower:** drag its grip onto any knob in Disperse, Distortion or Feedback.
- **Channel links** per section: edit both channels at once or one alone.
- **Band cards** with each band's real impulse response.
- **Dice** per section and for everything.
- **Factory presets:** Init (neutral), Buzzybeez, Fallguy, Keychains, Log.
- The GA bottom row: loudness-matched output, equal-loudness bypass, Dry/Wet and Bass Mono with locks,
  oversampling, Phase Protect, resizable window.
