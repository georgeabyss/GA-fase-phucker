Where it goes

VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
Your own presets are saved in ~/Music/GA/Fase Phucker/Presets.

After installing

Restart your DAW, or let it rescan its plug-ins. In Logic, the Audio Unit is under Audio Units > GA.

Updating

Just run the new installer, it replaces the old version. Your presets stay.

Uninstalling

Delete Fase Phucker.vst3 and Fase Phucker.component from the folders above.
