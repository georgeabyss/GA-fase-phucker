Fase Phucker

An all-pass disperser by GA: up to 1000 all-pass stages per band, with distortion, feedback and an envelope follower.

On the next pages you can choose what to install:

  • VST3 for Ableton Live, Bitwig, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, FL Studio and others
  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage

Needs macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
